<h2 align="center">🚀 MY PROJECTS</h2>

### 01. 📊 HR Employee Attrition Analysis

**Objective:** Analyze employee attrition patterns and identify high-risk employee segments.

**Key Findings**
- Identified an overall attrition rate of **16.17% across approximately 1.4K employees**.
- Identified R&D with 127 exits and Sales with 90 exits as priority departments.
- Found employees aged 26–35 to be the highest-risk age group, with 111 exits.
- Analyzed attrition by department, age group, salary band, and education field.

**Tools & Skills**

`Power BI` `DAX` `KPI Analysis` `Data Visualization`

---

### 02. 📈 Marketing Campaign Performance & ROI Analysis

**Objective:** Evaluate marketing campaign performance and identify opportunities to improve marketing efficiency.

**Key Findings**
- Evaluated **₹42.54M in revenue against ₹25.69M in spend** across 8 campaigns and 6 regions.
- Compared campaign performance using KPI cards and DAX measures.
- Identified Influencer Marketing as the top-performing campaign by ROI.
- Compared digital and traditional campaigns, which represented 62.5% and 37.5% of campaigns, respectively.
- Recommended budget reallocation based on spend-to-revenue efficiency.

**Tools & Skills**

`Power BI` `DAX` `KPI Reporting` `Marketing Analytics`

---

### 03. 🎯 Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Strategy

**Objective:** Analyze customer churn behavior and identify opportunities to improve customer retention.

**Key Findings**
- Analyzed a dataset of **2,000 subscribers**.
- Found that 77.9% of churned subscribers left before month six.
- Found that active customers were worth 3.7 times as much as churned customers, based on the analysis.
- Flagged 124 active subscribers as high churn risk using logistic regression.
- Achieved **85.8% ROC-AUC** for the churn prediction model.
- Examined the relationship between churn, satisfaction scores, and support-ticket volume.

**Tools & Skills**

`Python` `SQL` `Machine Learning` `Logistic Regression` `Customer Analytics`

---

### 04. 🛒 Retail Sales Performance Analysis

**Objective:** Investigate retail sales performance, profitability, and differences between stores.

**Key Findings**
- Analyzed **108,000 transactions across 50 stores**, representing ₹29.05 crore in revenue.
- Quantified a 20.3% weekend revenue-per-day shortfall.
- Found that discounts reduced the observed profit margin from 37.0% to 14.2%.
- Identified a 195% revenue gap between the top-performing and bottom-performing stores.
- Recommended discount caps, loyalty offers, and a turnaround plan for Tier-3 stores.

**Tools & Skills**

`Data Analysis` `Retail Analytics` `KPI Analysis` `Business Insights`

---

### 05. 📦 Seasonal Inventory Planning Review

**Objective:** Evaluate inventory performance and identify opportunities to reduce stockouts and excess inventory.

**Key Findings**
- Analyzed 36 months of data covering 30 products.
- Estimated **$282K in lost revenue** associated with a 10.9% stockout rate concentrated in December.
- Identified more than $150K tied up in overstocked SKUs.
- Recommended category-specific seasonal reorder points instead of using a single annual benchmark.

**Tools & Skills**

`Inventory Analytics` `Trend Analysis` `Demand Planning` `Business Analysis`
